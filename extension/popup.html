QuickGrab 0.2.0

Send direct file downloads to the Windows app.

Incognito/InPrivate downloads sent to QuickGrab are saved in its desktop history and on disk. Closing the private window does not erase them.

Enable Allow in incognito (Chrome) or Allow in InPrivate (Edge) in this extension’s browser details.

Destination: your user folder → Downloads → QuickGrab.
Browser cookies are not imported. Unsupported downloads stay in the browser.
